Open my Real-Price Dinner Kit Download a copy

Opens in your browser, works on phone, tablet and computer. Nothing to install and no login.

Do this first (it takes about 10 minutes)

  1. Bookmark it. Save this page and the kit. On your phone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen, so it's one tap away at the store.
  2. Price your pantry once. Open tab 1, type in your store and the month, then enter what each item costs. Every dinner prices itself from your numbers.
  3. Pick three dinners. Open the week builder, choose your dinners, and see the checkout total before you leave the house.

What's inside

  • Price your pantry: 47 everyday items, entered once, re-used by every recipe.
  • 25 dinners: full step-by-step recipes with a cost-per-serving badge, a picky-eater tweak and a "freezes well" tag.
  • Week builder: set a budget, plan up to 7 dinners and get a shopping list that rounds up to whole packages.
  • Cost tracker: a printable one-page sheet, plus rows to price your own family favorites.
  • Playbook: pantry heroes, stretch-it tricks and how to re-price each month.
Good to know
  • Your prices and plan are saved in the browser and device you use. Clearing that browser's site data erases them.
  • Use Copy my prices on tab 1 to back them up, or to move them to another device.
  • Re-enter prices when you change stores or once a month, since grocery prices move.
  • Cook poultry and reheated leftovers to 165°F.
